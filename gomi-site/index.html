HOWDY!

I'm Cody. I'm an elementary school teacher tasked with molding young minds and championing the arts. While the classroom is deeply rewarding, it isn't a completely fulfilling outlet for my creative ambitions.

A lifelong Oregonian, I currently live in SE Portland with my wife, our five-year-old son, and two perfect cats. My passion for art is backed by years of formal academic study and practice. However, my current portfolio leans a bit too heavily on collegiate assignments and primary-school classroom decor rather than large-scale commercial triumphs. My goal is to bridge that gap.

I want to paint your space! Whether you have a dreary exterior wall, a graffiti-riddled surface begging for a renaissance, a restroom that feels just a touch too clinical, or just want to switch things up a little because you have the time and inspiration-I am at your service. Simple patterns, typography, and thoughtful color palettes aren't risky and add a lot of personality! I am also very down to collaborate on a specific design meant to capture passerby attention, or just fulfill your own creative ideas!

I am offering my labor entirely free of charge. My primary objective right now is to develop a professional portfolio. Ultimately, my plan is to start a business to supplement my teaching salary. Getting to know new folks and doing big paintings are two other things I enjoy also. Help me out, yeah?

No Surprises: You will receive digital mock-ups for your approval before a single drop of paint touches your property.

Safety Net: Should the final product fail to meet your expectations, I will cover it up, repaint it entirely, or personally fund the restoration.

Bottom Line: Please let me spice up your daily routine just a wee lil' bit and draw more people into your establishment. If you have a wall or space in mind, let's talk-I'd greatly appreciate any opportunity. Don't hesitate to email, call, or text!
